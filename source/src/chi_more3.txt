Yours isn't a snake!" and drank it. Meaning: doing something unnecessary that spoils the result. ¶ Story 2, 守株待兔 (from the philosopher Hán Fēizǐ): A farmer saw a rabbit run into a tree stump and die. He took it home and ate it. From then on, he stopped farming and waited by the stump for another rabbit. None came, and his fields were ruined. Meaning: relying on luck instead of effort. ¶ Story 3, 对牛弹琴: A musician played beautiful music to a cow, but the cow kept eating grass.
