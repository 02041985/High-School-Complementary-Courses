hook: Restaurant inspectors, building inspectors and quality control technicians all do the same thing: compare work to a standard. In intermediate CTS, you start working to standards like a professional and helping peers do the same.
sc: I can compare my work to a written standard or specification | I can conduct a structured peer review | I can find defects, identify causes and revise
v: standard :: An agreed level of quality that work must meet.
v: specification :: Exact, measurable details a product must match.
v: inspection :: Checking work carefully against a standard.
v: defect :: A flaw that causes work to fail a standard.
v: root cause :: The underlying reason a defect happened.
v: peer review :: A structured check of work by a classmate.
ts: Inspect and Improve || Quality task || How do professionals check work against standards and improve it?
tx: Part 1, Read the spec (10 minutes): Use the specification for your project. Examples: ¶ • Foods: Food safety standard: internal temperature of chicken at least 74 °C; plating within 5 minutes; portion 150 g ± 10 g. ¶ • Construction: Joints flush within 0.5 mm; diagonals within 2 mm (proves square); no visible glue. ¶ • Fashion: Seam allowance 1.5 cm; stitches 2.5 mm; no puckering; finished size ± 1 cm. ¶ • Computing: Page loads under 3 seconds; works on phone and desktop; all links work; alt text on images. ¶ Part 2, Inspection checklist (10 minutes): Turn the spec into a yes or no checklist with a measurement column. ¶ Part 3, Peer inspection (15 minutes): Swap projects. Inspect with the checklist. Record measurements, not opinions. ¶ Part 4, Root cause (10 minutes): For each defect, ask "Why?" up to five times to find the root cause (the "5 Whys"). ¶ Part 5, Revise (15 minutes): Fix what can be fixed. For what can't, write a process change for next time.
