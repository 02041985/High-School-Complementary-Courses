v: piano roll :: An editor showing MIDI notes as bars on a grid of pitch and time.
v: quantize :: To move notes to the nearest beat or grid line.
v: velocity :: How hard a MIDI note is played, which affects volume and tone.

ts: Notes Without Sound || Production task || How do producers use MIDI to build arrangements?
tx: Tools: a DAW (GarageBand, BandLab, Soundtrap or similar) and a MIDI keyboard or the on-screen keyboard. ¶ Part 1, What MIDI is (5 minutes): MIDI stores note number (pitch), velocity (1–127), start time and length. No audio. ¶ Part 2, Record (15 minutes): Set a metronome at 90 bpm and a one-bar count-in. Record a four-bar chord part (C-G-Am-F) on a piano instrument. Then record a bass line on a new track. ¶ Part 3, Edit (15 minutes): Open the piano roll. Fix wrong notes by dragging. Quantize to 1/8 notes (try 75% strength to keep some feel). Raise velocity on beat 1 of each bar for accent. ¶ Part 4, Swap sounds (5 minutes): Change the chord track from piano to strings, then to a synth pad. Notice the same notes sound completely different. ¶ Part 5, Drums and mix (10 minutes): Program a drum pattern in step input (kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4, hi-hats on eighths). Balance levels: drums and bass firm, chords lower, melody clear. Pan hi-hats slightly.
